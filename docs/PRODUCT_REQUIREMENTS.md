# Product Requirements

## Business Goal

Build Closira into a trusted fashion utility that improves wardrobe visibility, outfit decision-making, reuse, and shopping confidence while protecting private personal images.

## Target Users

- Primary: women and girls with large wardrobes, college students, working professionals, brides, wedding shoppers, fashion-conscious users, influencers, boutique customers.
- Secondary: stylists, boutiques, makeup artists, clothing brands, jewelry brands, and families planning wedding outfits.

## Personas

- College planner: wants quick outfit choices and weekly planning.
- Working professional: needs office, formal, and daily wear organization.
- Wedding shopper: tracks traditional wear, jewelry, makeup, and event looks.
- Creator: needs styled outfit combinations and photo-ready planning.
- Boutique partner: wants future brand and stylist dashboards.

## User Problems

- Forgotten clothing items and repeated purchases.
- No central view of clothes, accessories, footwear, jewelry, and makeup.
- Difficulty matching colors, materials, occasions, and styles.
- Low visibility into unused clothes and cost per wear.
- Privacy concerns around personal photos and try-on images.

## Core Use Cases

- Register, verify email, set up profile, and configure privacy.
- Upload wardrobe items with front, back, and close-up images.
- Categorize items by default or custom categories and tags.
- Search, filter, sort, edit, favorite, and delete wardrobe items.
- Build complete outfits and mark them worn.
- Plan outfits for dates, trips, weddings, college, and office weeks.
- Get AI tagging suggestions and edit before saving.
- Compare a new purchase photo against existing wardrobe items.
- Receive AI outfit recommendations using actual owned items.

## MVP Scope

MVP includes authentication, profile, wardrobe CRUD, image upload, categories, tags, search, filters, outfit builder, calendar, and usage analytics. AI begins after the core data model and upload pipeline are stable.

## Premium Features

- Premium: unlimited wardrobe items, AI auto tagging, AI outfit recommendations, outfit calendar, usage analytics, similar clothing detection.
- Pro: virtual try-on, makeup try-on, jewelry try-on, advanced AI stylist, smart shopping assistant, event styling.
- Enterprise: boutique integration, brand dashboard, stylist dashboard, affiliate recommendations.

## Roadmap Summary

Phase 0 documents the architecture. Phase 1 scaffolds the monorepo. Phases 2-6 ship core app workflows. Phases 7-10 add AI. Phase 11 adds admin. Phase 12 adds virtual try-on foundation. Phase 13 hardens production.

## Success Metrics

- Activation: users add at least 10 wardrobe items in the first week.
- Engagement: weekly outfit planning and wardrobe search usage.
- Retention: monthly active users and repeated mark-worn events.
- Value: reduction in duplicate purchase confirmations and increased cost-per-wear insight.
- Trust: account deletion, image deletion, and consent flows work reliably.
