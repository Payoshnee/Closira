# Website Information Architecture

## Public Navigation

- Home: product promise, visual preview, major benefits, and primary CTA.
- Features: wardrobe, outfits, calendar, AI stylist, shopping assistant, analytics, and future try-on.
- How it works: step-by-step product workflow.
- AI Stylist: AI recommendations grounded in owned wardrobe items.
- Smart Shopping: duplicate purchase checks and wardrobe-aware buying decisions.
- Pricing: launch pricing and product plan framing.
- Contact: early-access and support contact form.

## Utility Routes

- Privacy: privacy-first wardrobe, photo, AI, storage, and consent policies.
- Terms: acceptable use and account terms.
- Login: account entry placeholder.
- Signup: early-access account creation placeholder.
- Forgot password: password reset request placeholder.

## Deferred Navigation

Authenticated dashboard routes are intentionally excluded from Run 1 and will be introduced in Run 2.
