# Closira
Closira is an AI-powered digital wardrobe and virtual styling app that helps users manage their clothes, remember what they own, create outfit combinations, and virtually try clothes, jewelry, makeup, and accessories before wearing them.  Users can upload front and back photos of their clothes, categorize them by type, material, occasion, color, and style, and then receive AI-based outfit suggestions for college, office, weddings, parties, festivals, travel, and daily wear.  The app solves a common problem where users own many clothes but forget what they have, repeat purchases, struggle to decide outfits, and waste time before events. Closira acts as a personal fashion assistant, helping users organize their closet, save money, reuse clothes better, and style themselves confidently.
